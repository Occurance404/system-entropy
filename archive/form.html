1. Title of the Project:
Quantifying Cognitive Collapse: An Entropic Framework for Stress-Testing and Rescuing Autonomous AI Agents

2. Problem Statement:
While Large Language Models (LLMs) demonstrate high proficiency in short-term tasks, they exhibit a phenomenon known as "Cognitive Collapse" or "Context Rot" during long-horizon, multi-step workflows. Current benchmarks measure binary success/failure but fail to quantify how an agent's reasoning degrades over time or when subjected to environmental "shocks" (e.g., changing requirements mid-task).

3. Objectives:

  1. Develop a Novel Metric Suite: To validate and standardize two new metrics: Semantic Collapse Ratio (SCR) and Regressive Debt Index (RDI).
  2. Stress-Test SOTA Models: To subject leading models to "Entropic Shock" experiments.
  3. Implement a Rescue Protocol: To demonstrate an automated "Handoff Mechanism".

4. Methodology:

  1. Framework Development: Developed a Python-based Orchestrator engine.
  2. Experiment Design: Utilizing a "Drug Discovery Filter" scenario.
  3. Data Collection: Running "Branching Probes" (5x parallel inference).
  4. Analysis: Correlating SCR/RDI with success rates.

5. Outcome:

  1. Publication: Scopus-indexed conference (AAAI, IJCAI).
  2. Dataset: Release of `entropic-shock` dataset.
  3. Tool: Open Source simulation framework.
